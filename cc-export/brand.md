## Type scale

| Level | Size | Weight | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display | 32–40px | Bold | Hero / cover titles |
| H1 | 24–28px | Bold | Section titles |
| H2 | 18–20px | Semibold | Subheads |
| Body | 15–16px | Regular | Paragraphs · UI copy |
| Caption | 12–13px | Regular | Meta · labels |

- **Heading face:** Plus Jakarta Sans Bold
- **Body face:** Plus Jakarta Sans Regular
- **Fallback:** system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif

## Logo rules

- **Clearspace:** Clearspace ~ half the mark height on all sides. Prefer full-color on cream; reverse on deep green covers.
- **Min size:** 28px digital · 0.6" print (mark height)

### Logo don’ts

- Do not stretch or rotate the mark
- Do not recolor outside green / gold / cream / near-black
- Do not place the mark on busy photography without a quiet field

## Imagery guidelines

- **Style:** Morning window light · timber and linen · documentary retail calm · cream fields · people unhurried at the counter
- **Do:** Hands pouring, shelf order, ceramic texture, soft shadows, real packaging in use
- **Don’t:** Neon cafe neon, staged “hustle” laptops, stock diversity handshakes, oversaturated beans

## Application note

Use the business card specimen as a proof of system: cover + quiet + accent + type pair + mark. Prefer roles over random swatches.

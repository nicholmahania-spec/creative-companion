# Harbor & Hearth

**BAKERS SINCE 2019**

Who: coastal pantry + roast shop. Feel: calm host. Goal: rebrand that reads craft without trend. Pillars: warmth, honesty, unhurried retail.

_Voice:_ Two counters, one oven, and a queue that starts before we open. We bake what the day needs and stop when it is gone.

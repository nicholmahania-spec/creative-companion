Direction sheet

Harbor & Hearth

BAKERS SINCE 2019

Positioning

Who: coastal pantry + roast shop. Feel: calm host. Goal: rebrand that reads craft without trend. Pillars: warmth, honesty, unhurried retail.

Voice

Two counters, one oven, and a queue that starts before we open. We bake what the day needs and stop when it is gone.

Palette
#8C4A2F · #D9A05B · #4A5D3A · #F3EBDD · #221A14
Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans Bold · Plus Jakarta Sans Regular

Logo direction

Harbor arc + hearth circle (mark) with Fraunces wordmark. Horizontal primary; mark alone for stamps and stickers. No drop shadows.

Do

Deep green + soft gold · cream paper fields · real window light · short CTAs · generous type · photography of hands, cups, shelves

Don't

Neon gradients · faux chalkboard · clipart beans · hustle slogans · dense product grids · more than one accent gold per layout

Mood direction
Morning counter — window light on cups
Shelf rhythm — cream labels, calm gaps
Ceramic + linen texture study
Quiet exterior — timber door, no neon
Cover green field
Accent gold for CTAs only
Open work
  • Star the 6 references going in the pack
  • Build the two strongest logo directions
  • Lock the palette and type pairing
  • Apply the system to the shop sign
  • Packaging labels and a bag
  • Export the logo pack — SVG, PNG, one-colour
  • Assemble the brand book and send it
